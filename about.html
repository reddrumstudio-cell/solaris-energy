Solar shouldn’t take a decade to make sense

One transparent monthly membership covers everything from initial setup to lifelong performance monitoring and maintenance. It’s the easiest way to power your home.

Solaris began in 2024 in Tampa, Florida, with two homeowners who were frustrated with their options.

The story behind the company

Maya Ortiz spent years in energy project finance before she priced out solar for her own house. Dan Reyes, a licensed electrician, had installed systems for other people and wanted one for himself. Both ran into the same offer: a multi-year loan, a lien on the property, messy subsidy filings, maintenance on their own dime, and a payback period of seven to ten years if everything went to plan.

Neither of them could justify it. They knew the technology worked. The deal was the problem.

So they built a different one.
